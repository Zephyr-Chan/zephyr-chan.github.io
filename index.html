About

I am an undergraduate student majoring in Software Engineering at the School of Electronic and Information Engineering, Wuyi University. My research interests center on AI Agents, Large Language Models (LLMs), Diffusion Models, and Brain-Computer Interfaces (BCIs).

During my studies, I have contributed to two research projects. The first is a multimodal data-driven automatic treatment planning Agent for intelligent radiotherapy, which integrates multi-source clinical data (CT images, dose distributions, OAR contours, and clinical protocols) to automate plan generation — building an Agent system with clinical decision-support capabilities to optimize the planning workflow. The second focuses on a topology-aware and mask-pretrained EEG foundation model, working to improve decoding accuracy and cross-subject generalization in brain-computer interface systems.

I plan to pursue an MPhil in Computer Science, where systematic research training can further strengthen my theoretical foundation and practical innovation skills. Outside the lab, I enjoy building small creative projects on GitHub, turning ideas into interactive demos that deepen my understanding of cutting-edge methods.

I am actively seeking diverse academic collaboration opportunities. If you are interested in any form of collaboration, technical exchange, or discussion, please feel free to reach out via email or instant messaging. I also maintain academic growth columns on Zhihu and Xiaohongshu, where I share learning notes and research progress — hoping to help fellow learners and researchers build confidence and find direction on their academic and career paths.
